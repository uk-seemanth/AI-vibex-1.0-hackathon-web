AI VibeX 1.0 is a six-hour sprint where teams turn an on-the-spot problem into a working AI product. Presented by the third-year AIML department of Mangalore Institute of Technology & Engineering, it rewards the teams that vibe the hardest.

Every project must put AI at its core. Bring designers, coders and ML builders, and plan to ship something live before the judges walk over.

6 hours

An intense sprint to build something extraordinary with AI.

Team spirit

Collaborate, ideate and ship together under pressure.

AI-first

Schedule

Event timeline

Your 6-hour journey, mapped out minute by minute.

  1. 9:15 – 9:30 AM

    Reporting, desk setup & power/Wi-Fi verification

    Teams arrive, settle in, set up hardware and check power and Wi-Fi.

    Desk Setup
  2. 9:30 – 9:45 AM

    Keynote, rules & reveal of on-spot problem statements

    AIML Dept keynote, rules, the evaluation rubric, and the surprise challenges revealed.

    Challenge Reveal
  3. 9:45 – 10:00 AM

    Ideation & repository setup (the vibe begins)

    Brainstorm, plan the architecture, initialise the GitHub repo and split roles.

    Vibe Begins
  4. 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM

    Core Sprint 1: prompting, API integration & UI build

    Three hours to build: prompt engineering, LLM and AI API integration, core logic and a rapid UI.

    Core Sprint 1
  5. 1:00 – 1:45 PM

    Lunch break (staggered, networking)

    Recharge, network with faculty and peers, and align your afternoon goals.

    Networking Break
  6. 1:45 – 3:30 PM

    Core Sprint 2: feature freeze, debugging & deployment

    Lock features, debug, and deploy live on Vercel, Render or the cloud.

    Feature Freeze & Deploy
  7. 3:30 – 4:00 PM

    Live demos & speed judging

    A 2-minute pitch per desk, a live demo to the panel, jury scoring and wrap-up.

    Speed Judging
